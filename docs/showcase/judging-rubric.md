## 2. Scoring note — carbon tonnage is not a gate

**Carbon tonnage (kg→tCO₂e) is explicitly NOT a pass/fail gate on its own.** Per Lasco's multi-factor NBS success rule (`docs/plaud/2026-08-15-climate-biodiversity.md`, Decision @48:16–48:20), an NBS-style project is judged on **environmental + technical + social + cultural + financial factor together** — single-factor optimization produces perverse outcome. Tonnage enters only through factor measurement honesty (is the number method-transparent and time-boxed?) and community benefit (who moved it, who was paid fairly). A team with small honest kg can outscore a team with big unverified tonnage.

## 3. Scoring note — do not reward overclaiming

Judges must not award point for what the team explicitly refuses to claim. The pitch non-claims in `docs/pitch-3min.md` are binding context:

- No carbon-credit product / Article 6 / ITMOs
- No plastic-neutrality or plastic-credit certificate
- No "we fix DPWH corruption" / national flood-control reform
- No "book a truck" guarantee until phone number verified
- Rates are **reference** until field-reverified

If a pitch implies any of these, treat it as a measurement-honesty deduction, not bonus. Reward the opposite: explicit non-claims and labeled uncertainty (R36 §2: "explicit non-claims" is a judge-reward behavior; R39: own limits unprompted).

## 4. Score anchor (1–5) per factor

| Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded problem fit | Problem = entire climate crisis or borrowed global stat; no place, no segment. | Broad theme named (waste) but no specific user friction; metric missing or invented. | One place + one friction stated; segment named but contact evidence thin. | Local pain quantified, time-boxed; named user plus who already works the stream (informal recovery visible). | Judge can restate it in one breath after 20s: place + friction + metric + who feels it + who already recovers it (R40 §1 bar). |
| Implementability | Slide vision only; no demo; megaproject shape. | Demo claimed but vapor feature (booking without verified collector); no pilot log. | Live happy path runs (or backup video plays); some field contact; next test vague. | Working loop live: rate → collector → log; ≥3 field-verified collector or honestly labeled pending; clear 30–90-day test. | Loop + exportable log with verification label + backup demo ready + specific next milestone (R40 prototype floor met). |
| Community benefit + equity | Beneficiary = everyone/planet; informal worker erased or tokenized. | Benefit asserted via savior narrative; no consent protocol. | Defined beneficiary incl. collector/junkshop; consent noted. | Named partner; fair-rate language; worker voice with consent; do-no-harm evident. | Justice beat lands: transparency framed as fairer path for informal recovery; replicable by another campus/org; maladaptation check answered (R42 lens). |
| Measurement honesty | Unfalsifiable planetary claim; blended kg; credit/neutrality implied. | Some number but no method, date, or verification split. | Time-boxed number with basic method; minor blending of verified vs estimated. | Pilot window + place + N user + K kg split by verification level; method card; honest miss disclosed. | Full claim-ladder discipline (rung A–C primary; climate co-benefit order-of-magnitude with uncertainty stated); limits offered unprompted; survives hostile Q&A (R40 §3–4). |
| Sustainability beyond event | Dies after the Sep 26 showcase; no owner named. | Vague "we will continue." | Org window or continuity path sketched. | Concrete afterlife: AIS/SEEDS or org + MRF window for 90 day; owner assigned. | Credible next step beyond the room + owner + what lets another node replicate (R36 honest-scalability bar). |
