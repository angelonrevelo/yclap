## 3 · The CCC's own pitch structure — match the deck to it

From the Session 4 "Prepare" PDF posted in the Innovators GC
(`Youth CLAP (Innovators GC) · 2026-09-05 09:31`). This is the checklist mentors score
against:

| Part | What they look for |
|---|---|
| 1. Introduce yourself | Who you are, which school, said with confidence |
| 2. Define the problem | A clear, compelling problem with an explicit link to climate change |
| 3. Goal + title | A goal that is easy to remember, with the project title said out loud |
| 4. What you will do | **2–3** specific, realistic activities |
| 5. How you will know | The M&E plan, with measurable outcomes |
| 6. Call to action | A specific ask the audience can act on |

The participant template adds a 7th part, **contact information**. It maps to a
12-slide reference deck (Slide 1 intro · 2–3 background · 4 goal · 5–7 activities · 8–9
M&E · 10–12 resources). Mentor guide questions to pre-answer: "Are your objectives
SMART?", "What are you asking from [partners]?", "How will you know if you are
successful?", "What support do you need from us?"

**Inference:** `deck-7-slide.md` is built around a 60-second demo and has no explicit
call-to-action slide or presenter intro. Ivan's Sep 10 slide allocation lines up with
parts 2–5 but has no owner for 1 or 6.
